# AI Insights templates

Starting points a company can adapt before turning on AI Insights. Each exists in English and Vietnamese; the employee
notice also in Spanish and Chinese. The whole compliance pack (data inventory, EU DPIA, US notes, works councils) is in
[docs/compliance](../../compliance/README.md).

| Template | English | Tiếng Việt |
|---|---|---|
| Notice to employees | [employee-notice.en.md](employee-notice.en.md) | [employee-notice.vi.md](employee-notice.vi.md) · [Español](employee-notice.es.md) · [中文](employee-notice.zh.md) |
| Internal AI use policy | [ai-use-policy.en.md](ai-use-policy.en.md) | [ai-use-policy.vi.md](ai-use-policy.vi.md) |
| Impact assessment | [impact-assessment.en.md](impact-assessment.en.md) | [impact-assessment.vi.md](impact-assessment.vi.md) |

**Not legal advice.** These templates describe how workspace works and point to the rules that usually apply
(Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on personal data protection; for staff in the EU, the GDPR, in particular Article 88
on processing in the employment context, and the national rules adopted under it). Laws, works-council and
union agreements differ by country and change over time. Have them reviewed by someone qualified for your
jurisdiction before use.

Fill in everything in `[square brackets]`. Keep the settings described in the documents equal to the settings in
Admin › AI; if you change the policy, update the notice and tell staff.
