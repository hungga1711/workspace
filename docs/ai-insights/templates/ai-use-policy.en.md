# Internal AI use policy

> Template. Not legal advice. Adapt it to your company and have it reviewed. Fill in every `[bracket]`.

**Company:** [name] · **Owner of this policy:** [role] · **Version:** [1.0], [date] · **Review:** every [12] months

## 1. Purpose

AI tools can save time. They can also leak confidential data, produce wrong answers that look right, and cost money
nobody tracks. This policy says which tools we use, what may go into them, and how we check that they help.

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors and interns who use AI tools for [company] work, on company or personal devices.

## 3. Approved tools

| Tool | Account | Allowed for |
|---|---|---|
| Company AI in workspace (Trợ lý AI) | company connection | all work, including internal data classified up to [Internal] |
| [AI chat tool A] | [company business plan / personal accounts allowed] | [drafting, research, code, …] |
| [AI chat tool B] | [ … ] | [ … ] |

Other AI tools need approval from [role] before use for work.

## 4. Data rules

Never put into an AI tool outside the company AI:

- personal data of customers or colleagues (ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, health, salary);
- card numbers, bank accounts, passwords, API keys, private keys;
- [client names, project codes, unreleased financials, source code of …] (also in the keyword list in Admin › AI);
- anything marked [Confidential] or covered by an NDA.

The workspace sensitive-data filter is set to **[warn / redact / block]** and catches common cases; it does not replace your
judgement.

## 5. Using the output

- You are responsible for what you send, publish or ship, AI-assisted or not.
- Check facts, numbers, quotes, legal or medical statements and code before using them.
- Say when a customer-facing text or a decision document was mostly written by AI, where [our rules / the customer]
  require it.
- Do not use AI to make decisions about people (hiring, evaluation, discipline) without a human decision.

## 6. AI Insights

We use AI Insights in workspace to see what AI is used for, protect data and learn whether it helps. The notice to
employees ([link]) explains what is recorded, who sees it and your rights. In short: only prompts you send on listed
AI sites after you connect the extension; no screenshots or keystrokes; you see everything about you; managers see
[topic / topic and an AI-written summary], never the text of your prompts; kept [180] days.

AI Insights data is **not** used to rank or score individuals.

## 7. Sharing good prompts

Use "Share this prompt" in Me › My AI to add a useful prompt to the company library. Check the redacted text before you
share. People who often get help from AI may be asked to mentor colleagues; this is voluntary.

## 8. Cost

Company AI has monthly budgets per [company / department / person] set in Admin › AI. Personal paid subscriptions
for work are [reimbursed / not reimbursed] [conditions].

## 9. Incidents

If confidential or personal data went into an AI tool by mistake, tell [contact] within [24 hours]. Do not try to
"delete" it by asking the AI. We assess and, where the law requires, notify the authority (Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP sets
72 hours for notifying the authority of a personal-data breach; GDPR Art. 33 sets 72 hours for the EU).
