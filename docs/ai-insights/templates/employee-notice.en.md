## Why we are doing this

We use AI tools at work and pay for some of them. We want to know what we use them for, keep company and customer
data safe, and learn whether AI actually helps us get work done. We use **AI Insights** in workspace, our
self-hosted work app, for this.

## What is recorded

Only after you connect the workspace browser extension with your own code:

- the prompts you send on these AI chat sites: [list from the extension], at the moment you press send;
- [the answers, only if the box below says so] Answers captured: **[yes / no]**;
- the site, the model shown on the page, the time, a topic, and the kinds of sensitive data found;
- the task a conversation served, if you link it or confirm a suggestion;
- your answer to "Did AI help?", if you give one;
- when the sensitive-data filter warns, redacts or blocks a prompt: the site, the kind of data and the count, **not the text**.

Your use of the company AI inside workspace is recorded the same way.

## What is never recorded

Screenshots, keystrokes, idle or active time, other websites, browsing history, text you typed and deleted, and
anything before you connect the extension. Every page that is recorded shows a notice in the corner.

## Who sees what

- **You** see everything recorded about you in workspace › Me › My AI, including exactly what managers see.
- **Your manager** sees, for their reporting line: [topic only / topic and a one-line summary written by AI].
- **Administrators** ([roles or names]) see everyone at the same level. Nobody but you, administrators included,
  can read the text of your prompts.
- Nobody outside the company. Data is stored on [our own server / server operated by X for us] in [country].
  It is not sent to the workspace authors or to any other third party. [If a company AI model is connected:
  summaries are produced with our model connection at [provider], under our agreement with them.]

## Sensitive data

Before a prompt leaves your browser, the extension checks it for ID numbers, card numbers, secrets, bank accounts,
contact lists and our own confidential terms. Our setting is **[warn / redact / block]**: [warn: you are asked
before sending; redact: sensitive parts are replaced by placeholders you see before sending; block: the prompt is not
sent].
