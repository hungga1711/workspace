# Compliance pack

> **Not legal advice.** These documents describe how workspace works and the rules that usually apply. Laws,
> works-council and union agreements differ by country and change. Have counsel review them for your jurisdiction
> before you rely on them.

Everything a small company usually needs before turning on AI Insights for staff, in one place.

## Order of work

1. Fill in the data inventory with your settings (Admin › AI › Privacy shows the read policy, answer capture and
   retention).
2. EU/UK staff: do the DPIA and the works-council step **before** switching AI Insights on. US staff: check the state
   notice rules for where people work.
3. Send the employee notice in each person's language; collect acknowledgements where the law asks for them.
4. Switch on AI Insights; staff connect the extension themselves.
5. Review once a year, and whenever you change the read policy, capture answers, or add a model provider.
