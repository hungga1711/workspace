# Staff representatives in the EU

> Not legal advice. Whether and how representatives must be involved depends on the country, the size of the
> company and any collective agreement. Ask local employment counsel.

Monitoring tools usually need staff representatives to be informed or consulted, and in some countries their
agreement, **before** they go live. AI Insights records prompts on AI sites, which most regimes treat as a technical
means able to monitor employees, even when that is not the purpose.

| Country | Typical rule | What it means for AI Insights |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | Works Constitution Act (BetrVG) § 87(1) no. 6: co-determination on technical devices **suitable** to monitor behaviour or performance | A works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung) before go-live, if there is a works council. |
| Austria | Labour Constitution Act (ArbVG) § 96(1) no. 3: monitoring measures affecting human dignity need the works council's consent; § 96a for personnel data systems | Consent of the works council, or individual consent where there is none. |
| France | Labour Code L2312-38 (CSE consultation on monitoring tools) and L1222-4 (prior information of employees) | Consult the CSE and inform each employee before use. |
| Netherlands | Works Councils Act (WOR) art. 27(1)(l): consent for systems to monitor attendance, behaviour or performance | Works council consent. |
| Spain | Workers' Statute art. 64; Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) arts. 87–91 on digital rights at work | Inform workers' representatives; set criteria for use of digital devices with them; inform employees beforehand. |

## What to give the representatives

- The [data inventory](data-inventory.md) and the [DPIA](dpia-eu.md).
- The settings you will use: read policy (topic / summary), answers captured (yes / no), retention, sensitive-data
  mode, who has admin access.
- The employee notice in the languages your staff read.
- A commitment written into the agreement: no individual performance scoring from AI Insights; aggregated use for
  decisions about tools, training and policy; access logs available to the representatives on request.

## Suggested clauses for a works agreement

1. Purpose: data protection, tool and cost management, learning whether AI helps work. Not performance evaluation.
2. Scope: listed AI sites only, after each employee connects the extension; company AI inside workspace.
3. Visibility: managers see [topic / topic and one-line summary] only; nobody but the employee can read their
   prompts, and the employee sees exactly what managers see.
4. Retention: [180] days, automatic deletion.
5. Changes to these settings require [notice to / agreement of] the works council.
6. Review after [6 months] with the usage report.
