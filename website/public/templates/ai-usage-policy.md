# AI usage policy — [Company name]

_Version 1.0 · Effective [date] · Owner: [name, role] · Review every 6 months_

> Template from the Workspace guides. Not legal advice: adapt it to your company and have counsel review it.

## 1. Why we have this policy

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and similar) help us write, analyse and plan faster. They also
create risks: customer data leaving the company, confident but wrong answers, and work nobody can check later. This
policy says which tools we use, what never goes into them, and how we stay accountable for the result.

## 2. Approved tools

| Tool | Account type | Approved for |
|---|---|---|
| [e.g. ChatGPT Team] | Company workspace | All staff, general work |
| [e.g. Claude Team] | Company workspace | All staff, general work |
| [e.g. company AI in Workspace] | Company connection | Summaries, drafts, task helpers |

Personal accounts on free plans are **not** approved for work content, because the provider may use what you type
to train its models. Ask [owner] if you need a tool that is not on the list.

## 3. Never paste into any AI tool

- Customer personal data: names with contact details, ID or passport numbers, addresses, phone numbers.
- Payment data: card numbers, bank account numbers.
- Health, salary or performance information about any person.
- Passwords, API keys, private keys, access tokens.
- Contracts, prices or plans marked confidential, unless the tool is approved for them above.

If you are unsure, remove the details first ("Customer A", "€X") or ask.

## 4. Using the output

- You are responsible for anything you send, publish or decide with AI help. Check facts, numbers and names.
- Do not present AI output as a legal, medical or financial opinion.
- Say that AI helped when the reader would want to know (customer-facing reports, published articles).
- Keep a link between AI work and the task it belongs to, so the team can see what helped.

## 5. What the company records

We use [Workspace AI Insights] to understand how AI helps our work. It records [which tool, when, the topic and,
under our current read policy, a short summary] of conversations on the approved AI sites, after you connect the
browser extension. You can see everything recorded about you under Me › AI, and who has opened it. Sensitive data
such as card or ID numbers is [warned about / hidden / blocked] before it is sent.

We use this to decide where AI is worth paying for and where people need help, not to rate individuals.

## 6. Incidents

If customer or confidential data went into an AI tool by mistake, tell [owner or security contact] the same day. We
will check the provider's data controls and, where needed, ask the provider to delete it.
