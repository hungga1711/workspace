---
title: "Redact customer data before it reaches ChatGPT"
description: "Stop card numbers, ID numbers and customer contact details from being pasted into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini — warn, hide or block in the browser before the prompt is sent."
---

The safest customer data is data that never leaves the browser. Workspace's browser extension checks each prompt on
the approved AI sites **before it is sent** and applies the company's rule for sensitive data.

## Three modes

| Mode | What the person sees | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Warn | A notice naming what was found; they can edit or send anyway | The prompt as written; the event is logged |
| Hide (redact) | The prompt with matches replaced by placeholders such as `[EMAIL]` | The redacted prompt |
| Block | The send is stopped with the reason | Nothing |

Admins choose the mode in Admin › System › AI connections › Privacy. Every warning, redaction and block is counted in the weekly report,
so you can see whether people need a reminder or a better tool.

## What it looks for

Email addresses, phone numbers, payment card numbers (checked with the Luhn rule), bank account formats, and national
ID formats such as US SSNs, UK National Insurance numbers and Vietnamese citizen IDs, plus keywords your company adds
(project code names, customer names). Patterns for more countries are added over time; check the
[extension documentation](/docs/ai-insights/extension) for the current list.

## Why in the browser

Network tools see encrypted traffic, or require breaking it open. The extension runs where the text is typed, only
on the AI sites listed, only after the person connects it, and with a notice on every page. It never sends your data
to the Workspace project: events go to your own server.

## Pair it with a policy

Tools catch patterns; people catch context. Publish a one-page
[AI usage policy](/docs/guides/ai-usage-policy-template) that says what never goes into AI, and use the weekly
report to follow up.
