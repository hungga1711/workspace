title: "How to measure AI ROI at work"
description: "Count less, compare more. A practical way for a small company to see whether AI shortens real work — with sample sizes, without spreadsheets, and without rating people."
---

Most "AI ROI" numbers are guesses: minutes saved per prompt times prompts. They measure activity, not results. For
a small company there is a simpler, more honest test: **do tasks done with AI finish faster or more often on time
than similar tasks done without it?**

## What to measure

| Measure | Why it matters | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| People using AI each week | Adoption, and who is left out | Extension, company AI, imports |
| Tasks done with AI vs without | The base for every comparison | Conversations linked to tasks |
| Time to done (median days) | Is the work shorter? | Task history |
| On-time rate | Is the work more reliable? | Due dates and completion |
| Cost | Is it worth paying for? | Provider usage and prices |

## Rules that keep it honest

- **Compare like with like.** Same team, same period, tasks of the same kind where you can.
- **Show sample sizes.** A difference between 4 and 6 tasks is noise. Workspace shows no comparison until each side
  has at least 20 tasks, and prints the counts next to every figure.
- **Correlation is not causation.** People who use AI may be your fastest people anyway. Read the trend over weeks,
  and look at the same person before and after.
- **Measure teams, not individuals.** The goal is to decide where to invest and where to help, not to rank people.

## Link the work

The comparison only works when AI conversations are linked to tasks. Workspace does most of it for you: when a
prompt mentions a task code (T-157), the extension and Me › AI offer the link in one click; workflow runs and the
in-app assistant are linked automatically. You can also [import ChatGPT or Claude Team history](/docs/ai-insights)
to start with data you already have.

## Read it weekly

The weekly AI report (AI › AI adoption › Weekly report, and an optional Monday e-mail an admin turns on) lists who
used AI, what for, the with/without comparison when there is enough data, what the sensitive-data net caught, cost,
and three things to do next.
