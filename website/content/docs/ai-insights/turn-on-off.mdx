---
title: Turn AI on or off
description: AI is optional and off by default. Two separate switches control it, and the rest of Workspace never depends on either.
---

A new workspace has no AI at all: no assistant, no AI buttons, nothing recorded. Chat, tasks, documents, meetings
and approvals work fully without it. An admin can switch on either of two independent parts, at any time, and switch
them off again.

| Switch | What it turns on | Needs an AI model? |
|---|---|---|
| **The company's own AI** | The assistant, @AI in chat, summaries, translation, task and document help, agents | Yes, one you connect (your key, an OpenAI-compatible API, or a self-hosted model) |
| **AI Insights** | Recording how staff use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot… through the browser extension, the safety net and the reports | No; a model only adds one-line summaries |

## Where the switches are

- **At first setup**: `/setup` has a section *AI (optional, off by default)* with both choices, unticked.
- **Any time later**: **Admin › System › AI connections**. The two switches are at the top of its *Connections*
  tab; *Insights*, *Privacy*, *Cost & limits* and *Danger zone* are the other tabs. Only admins (the Owner and Admin
  roles) see it.

Each switch saves as soon as you change it. Whatever you leave off stays hidden from everyone.

## Turning on the company's own AI

<Steps>
<Step>
Switch on **The company's own AI**.
</Step>
<Step>
Further down the *Connections* tab, add a connection:

- **API key**: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini);
- **OpenAI-compatible API**: any server that speaks that API;
- **Self-hosted**: Ollama or LM Studio on your own machine (for example `http://host.docker.internal:11434`).

Press **Test**, then **Make default**. Keys are encrypted before they are stored.
</Step>
<Step>
Optional, in *Cost & limits*: enter each model's price and set monthly budgets for the company, a department or a
person. Calls stop with
a clear message when a budget is reached.
</Step>
</Steps>

Operators can instead set `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `GEMINI_API_KEY` or `OPENAI_BASE_URL` in `.env`
(see [Configuration](/docs/self-hosting/configuration)); the switch still decides whether people see AI.

## Turning on AI Insights

<Steps>
<Step>
Switch on **AI Insights**. The policy and safety net are in the *Privacy* tab; topics, the weekly report e-mail and
provider imports in *Insights*.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the policy: how much managers may read (topic only, a one-line summary, or full text), whether answers are kept,
how long data is kept (7 to 730 days, 180 by default), and the safety-net mode (warn, redact or block) with your own
keywords. Everyone is notified of each policy change.
</Step>
<Step>
Tell staff before recording starts; templates for the notice and an internal AI policy are in the
[repository](https://github.com/hungga1711/workspace/tree/main/docs/ai-insights/templates).
</Step>
<Step>
Each person installs the extension and connects it with a code from **Me › AI**. Nothing is recorded for anyone who
has not connected it. Details: [Browser extension](/docs/ai-insights/extension).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Turning AI off again

| You switch off | What happens |
|---|---|
| **The company's own AI** | The assistant and every AI button disappear for everyone. Tasks, docs and chat are unchanged. |
| **AI Insights** | Recording stops at once: installed extensions show *not connected* and send nothing. Data already recorded stays until its retention ends. |

To remove recorded data immediately, use **Delete all AI Insights data** in the *Danger zone* tab. It deletes every recorded
conversation, who opened them, and every extension connection code. The company's own AI usage records are not
affected. Each person can also export their own data or ask for deletion in **Me › AI**.
