AI Insights covers the company's own AI inside Workspace and popular AI chat tools in the browser, recorded by the
Workspace extension. The rules behind it are in
[ADR 0020](https://github.com/hungga1711/workspace/blob/main/docs/adr/0020-ai-insights-principles.md); notice and
policy templates are in
[docs/ai-insights/templates](https://github.com/hungga1711/workspace/tree/main/docs/ai-insights/templates).

## Recorded and never recorded

| Recorded | Never recorded |
|---|---|
| The prompt, when the person presses send | Screenshots or screen video |
| The answer, only if the policy captures answers (off by default) | Keystrokes, typing speed, mouse movement |
| Site, time, topic, sensitive-data kinds | Idle time or "productivity" scores |
| The task it served, once the person confirms | Other websites, tabs or history |
| "Did AI help?" answers | Text the safety net redacted or blocked |

## Turn it on

Off by default. An admin switches it on and sets the policy; each person connects the
[extension](/docs/ai-insights/extension) with their own code, and nothing is recorded before that. Step by step:
[Turn AI on or off](/docs/ai-insights/turn-on-off).

## Who sees what

| Screen | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Me › AI** | Each person | Their week by topic, tasks AI helped with, their own warnings, every recorded conversation and who opened it; export (JSON) and deletion request |
| **AI › AI adoption › Usage** | Managers (their reporting line), admins (everyone) | Tools, topics, people, departments, cost, safety-net counts, and tasks done with AI help against without |

Managers read only what the policy allows: `topic`, a one-line `summary` (default) or `full` text. Opening a full
conversation is logged and shown to the person.

## Safety net

The extension checks each prompt in the browser before it is sent, for personal ID numbers, card and bank details,
secrets, contact lists and the company's own keywords, and acts as the company chose:

| Mode | What the person sees |
|---|---|
| `warn` (default) | A warning; send anyway or cancel |
| `redact` | Sensitive parts replaced with `[CCCD]`, `[CARD]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[SECRET]`… before sending |
| `block` | The prompt is not sent |

Only the event is stored (person, site, action, kinds, count), never the redacted text. Detection is heuristic: it
catches common patterns, not everything.

## Does AI help?

Conversations are linked to tasks by the person, by a hint from the task they had open (confirmed by the person),
or by a full-text suggestion. AI › AI adoption › Usage then compares tasks done with AI help against the rest: done, on time,
median days to done. After a conversation the extension asks "Did AI help? yes / no / partly", one tap, optional.
This is a correlation, and the page says so.

## Also included

Topics (keyword-based, refined by a connected model), a shared prompt library, a skills map of likely mentors, import
of usage exports from AI vendors' admin consoles, and a one-page weekly report. Everything works without an AI
model; with one connected, it adds one-line summaries and model-based topics.

## Retention and deletion

Conversations older than the policy's retention are deleted hourly. Each person can export their data and ask for
deletion in Me › AI. Admins can delete all AI Insights data in Admin › System › AI connections › Danger zone.
